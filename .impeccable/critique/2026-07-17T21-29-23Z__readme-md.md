# University Quad Asset Pack Critique

**Target:** `C:\Users\Summit E16 Flip\Desktop\Claude\Asset Bank\University Quad Asset Pack`
**Date:** 2026-07-17
**Design health:** 20/40

## Verdict

The pack is a strong concept and art-direction prototype. The isometric composition, palette, building silhouette, and campus-board readability are on the right path. It is not yet a production-ready runtime handoff: the board models five departments instead of six, the student atlas contains cross-cell clipping, anchors do not hold a stable ground point, and the document lacks a numeric integration contract.

## Strengths

- Clear square-board hierarchy with readable paths, lawns, fountain, and building pads.
- Cohesive brick, limestone, charcoal-roof, muted-green visual language.
- Useful building silhouette differentiation in the overview.
- The concept communicates the intended SimCity/RollerCoaster Tycoon campus atmosphere.

## P1 — Fix before implementation handoff

1. **Five-pad board omits Marketing.** The terrain, overview, prompts, and building roster encode five departments. Safety School requires six.
2. **Character atlas crosses frame boundaries.** Three jog cells contain fragments of the following row; idle and frisbee poses are clipped. Individual frame exports reproduce the same defect.
3. **Character anchor is unstable.** The single `(128,240)` anchor does not normalize feet or seated/action poses, causing visible sliding and jumping.
4. **No numeric runtime contract.** Board origin, pad coordinates, scale, pivots, footprints, z-sort keys, occlusion regions, and HUD-safe viewport bounds are unspecified.
5. **Campus condition and construction states are absent.** Prosperity, strain, austerity, build-preview, dust/rise, and completion feedback have no minimum asset or procedural contract.
6. **Fountain plan contradicts its export.** The handoff calls for procedural water, but the sprite already bakes the surface and jet into one layer.
7. **Viewport and board interaction states are undefined.** The pack does not prove full-board fit at 1280×720 and 1024×768 or define hover, focus, selected, valid, invalid, and completion states.

## P2 — Resolve during production hardening

- Despill the remaining magenta fringe: 32.78% of the building's and 25.07% of the fountain's measured partial-alpha boundary pixels exceeded the magenta threshold.
- Define contact-shadow ownership. Isolated assets omit shadows while the overview depends on them for grounding.
- Separate runtime, source, and concept payloads; document or remove the unreferenced `student-actions-sheet.png` and choose atlas or individual frames as canonical.
- Add texture budgets, trimming rules, sampling/gutter policy, color metadata, license/provenance, and checksums.
- Strengthen department-specific silhouettes and authored environmental storytelling to reduce the polished-but-generic AI city-builder look.

## Recommended vertical-slice contract

1. Revise the board and handoff to six named pads. Reusing one generic building with runtime labels is acceptable for the slice.
2. Re-export a minimal clean character set with genuine loop phases, transparent cell gutters, and per-frame pivots.
3. Add a machine-readable runtime manifest for board, pad, building, prop, character, and depth metadata.
4. Keep the fountain static in the slice; split stone, water/mask, and foreground rim only when procedural water is scheduled.
5. Define one minimal construction effect and a three-state prosperity/strain/austerity cue matrix.
6. Require one complete solo round at both target resolutions using HTML/CSS placeholders for unfinished art.
